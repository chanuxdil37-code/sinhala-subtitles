2 · Engine

Sinhala Whisper is a version of Whisper trained on Sinhala speech, so it writes proper Sinhala letters. It downloads once (~290 MB) and the browser keeps it. It runs on your computer's processor, so a 10-minute video can take 10–20 minutes. Check the text before you export.
Get one free at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Stored only in this browser.
